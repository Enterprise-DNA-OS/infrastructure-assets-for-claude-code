---
description: A renewal business case for one asset or a group of assets, written to drafts/.
---

1. Run `npm run assets -- asset <ASSET> --json` for each asset, and `npm run assets -- funding-gap --years=10 --json`.
2. Write `drafts/business-case-<asset>.md`: the asset and the service it gives, condition history, criticality, open defects, the forecast renewal cost, the risk of deferring (what fails and who is affected), options (renew now, defer with monitoring, staged), and the year it should go in.
3. Every fact from the records. Anything the committee will want that is not in the records (a quote, an engineer's report) goes at the end as "to attach".
4. Show the draft. Nothing is sent.
