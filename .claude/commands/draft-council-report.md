---
description: A committee or council report on the state of the assets, written to drafts/.
---

1. Run `npm run assets -- condition --json`, `ratios --json`, `backlog --json`, `funding-gap --years=30 --json` and `compliance --json`.
2. Write `drafts/asset-report-YYYY-MM-DD.md` in the council's report shape: purpose, summary, the condition of the network, the ratios against their benchmarks with sources, the 30-year funding gap, risks, and recommendations for the operator to edit.
3. Plain language for elected members. Every figure from the commands.
4. Show the draft. Nothing is sent or published.
