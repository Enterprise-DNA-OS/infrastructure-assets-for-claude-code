description: The assets due for renewal, year by year, and whether a works item is planned for each.
---

1. Run `npm run assets -- renewals --years=10 [--class=CLASS] --json`.
2. Show year, asset, class, area, condition, criticality, cost and the planned works item.
3. List separately the critical assets (4 or 5) due in the next three years with nothing planned: those are the decisions to bring to the next budget.
