description: Renewals, essential services, maintenance and backlog ratios for last year, this year and the next two.
---

1. Run `npm run assets -- ratios --json` and `npm run assets -- settings --json` for the jurisdiction.
2. New Zealand: lead with the essential services benchmark (capital spend on network services at least equal to their depreciation, Financial Reporting and Prudence Regulations 2014 reg 20). NSW: lead with the renewals ratio (100% or more), backlog ratio (under 2%) and maintenance ratio (100% or more).
3. Say which years are actual and which are budget. Note that depreciation is today's register for every year.
